more than a moment.

My dearest Menna,

Happy birthday to the person who makes the world feel a little softer just by being in it. I hope this new year brings you the kind of happiness that stays — the quiet kind that finds you in ordinary moments and reminds you how deeply loved you are.

Thank you for being exactly who you are: for your warmth, your laughter, and the way you make even simple moments feel worth remembering. Having you in my life has changed the way I see everything. You make the imperfect days gentler, the good days brighter, and the future something I look forward to.

I hope you always see yourself with some of the tenderness I see in you. You deserve every beautiful thing that comes your way, every reason to smile, and a year filled with little surprises that feel like they were made just for you.

Whatever this next chapter brings, I hope you never forget how special you are to me. I am grateful for every memory we have made and excited for all the little moments still waiting for us.

With all the words I could find, and all the feelings they could never fully hold.
With love, always ♡
